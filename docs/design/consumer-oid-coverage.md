## Per starter walk

`Group present` is the measure that matters: the walk serves something in
the MIB group the instrument asked in, so the object it came looking for
is on the device. `Verbatim` additionally requires the exact instance it
asked this network's devices for -- an ifIndex, an ARP entry keyed by the
manager's own address -- which a capture of a different network cannot
hold. Verbatim is shown to size that gap, never as a target.

Group presence rewards breadth over depth on purpose, which is why a
588-row walk can outrank a 23,067-row one here and lose badly on verbatim:
the two columns answer different questions and a pack needs both.
